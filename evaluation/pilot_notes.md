# SkillRadar — Pilot Test Notes

**Participants:** 2 classmates (P01 and P02), approximately 15 minutes each.

**Method:** Think aloud. Participants upload a CV or sample CV, confirm/correct extracted tools, inspect the skill-gap report, and choose a tool to learn next.

**Privacy:** Do not record names, emails, or CV contents here. Ask for consent before each session. If any external AI processing is used, disclose it and obtain separate consent.
