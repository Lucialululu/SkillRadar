# SkillRadar — User Study Protocol

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this user study is to evaluate whether SkillRadar is understandable, useful, and trustworthy for students and recent graduates who are looking for jobs.

SkillRadar allows users to upload their CV, automatically identifies tools and technologies mentioned in it, and compares them with tools requested in job postings for their chosen role.

We want to investigate three main questions:

1. **Perceived accuracy:** Do users think SkillRadar correctly identifies their tools and missing tools?
2. **Usefulness:** Does SkillRadar help users decide which tools or technologies they should prioritize learning?
3. **Trust:** Do users understand and trust the information and recommendations provided by SkillRadar?

We will also observe whether users can complete the main tasks without assistance and whether they encounter difficulties using the interface.

## 2. Participants

We will conduct an initial pilot test with 2 classmates to identify usability problems.

After improving the prototype based on the pilot results, we plan to conduct a larger peer trial with 6–8 students.

Participation will be voluntary.
