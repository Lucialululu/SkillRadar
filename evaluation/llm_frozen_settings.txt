Settings of the frozen LLM evaluation (git tag llm-v1-frozen, 4 Nov 2026).
Copy these lines into your .env (next to GEMINI_API_KEY) to reproduce evaluation/results/goal1_test_*.md.
Cached answers live in data/llm_cache/ on the machine that ran it (gitignored).

SKILLRADAR_LLM_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash-lite
SKILLRADAR_EMBED_MODEL=gemini-embedding-001
SKILLRADAR_LINK_THRESHOLD=1.0
SKILLRADAR_FEWSHOT=post_003,post_005

Chosen on the dev split (evaluation/tune_llm.py, 8 dev postings without the two few-shot items):
linking off gave the best F1 (all) for both LLMOnly (0.93) and Hybrid (0.92); every threshold that allowed
embedding links lowered precision and recall.
After the freeze only retry handling changed (timeouts are retried); answers and scoring are unchanged.
